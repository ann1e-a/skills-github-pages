## Lessons Learned So Far
1. **Case Sensitivity Matters:** `profile.md` is not `PROFILE.md`. The bot holds grudges over lowercase letters.
2. **Spacing is Essential:** Markdown elements need breathing room. If you don't use blank lines, your layout looks like a single continuous sentence.
3. **It’s a Feature:** If an exercise doesn't work the first time, I am simply stress-testing the robustness of the course architecture.
